<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-to-expect">What to expect:</h2>



<p>AI tools and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) are changing how developers build, debug, and manage WooCommerce stores. We want to hear from <em>you</em>—how are you using AI in your Woo development and store management workflows? What&#8217;s working? What&#8217;s frustrating? Where should we focus next?</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-join">Why join?</h2>



<p>This is your chance to share what you&#8217;re experimenting with and learn from others in the community. Whether you&#8217;re using AI assistants for code generation, leveraging MCP servers for store data, automating support workflows, or just curious about what&#8217;s possible—bring your questions, wins, and pain points.</p>



<p>We&#8217;re listening. Your input will help shape how we support AI-powered development in the WooCommerce ecosystem.</p>



<p><strong>Don’t miss out—join us on April 15!</strong></p>
